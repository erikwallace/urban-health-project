Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects and how it's used.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or submit a form to use this site. The only information we collect is:

  • Analytics data, if you accept analytics cookies (see below): pages visited, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, and the site that referred you here. This comes from Google Analytics.
  • Anything you send us directly, such as your name and email address if you email us at urbanhealthproject@gmail.com.

Cookies

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you're okay with analytics cookies. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish you from other visitors. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics script loads. Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie, and you can change it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for urbanhealthproject.org and reloading the page.

Google Fonts

This site uses fonts from Google's servers. Google may log your IP address when your browser downloads a font file.

Your Choices

You can decline analytics cookies in the banner when you first visit, or change your choice later by clearing your browser's site data for this domain. Declining doesn't limit anything you can do on this site.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at urbanhealthproject@gmail.com.
